Frequently asked questions

MachiPower is designed for university students who want to join campus or intercollegiate competitions and are looking for suitable teammates. The platform is currently open to students with a valid university email address.

After registering and logging in, you can create your profile, upload your resume, browse competition lists, and mark the competitions you are interested in. You can set the skills you expect from teammates for each competition, and the system will automatically recommend the best matches based on your preferences.

MachiPower makes it easy to find teammates with the right skills, streamlines the team formation process, and increases your chances of building a strong team for competitions. The platform automates matching, making the process transparent and efficient.

No, MachiPower is a web-based platform and requires an active internet connection to access features such as registration, profile editing, competition browsing, and team matching.

Your data is securely stored using AWS services such as Cognito for authentication, DynamoDB for user and competition data, and S3 for resume files. Only relevant information is shared with potential teammates during the matching process, and your personal data is never made public without your consent.

After receiving your recommended matches, you can send invitations to join your team. You can also manage invitations you receive, with options to accept or decline directly on the platform.

Currently, you can only select skills from the platform’s preset list to ensure consistency and accurate matching.

You can request account deletion through your profile settings or by contacting customer support. All your data will be permanently removed from the platform.
